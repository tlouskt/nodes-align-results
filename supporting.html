About this results snapshot

This publication contains 65 completed family plots from 27 October 2026, 8:40 PM Pacific. Each contains all five Qwen sizes with ProtT5 and ESM3 comparisons. Completion does not mean every numerical check passed.

Use “Download completed values” on the plots page to save the displayed values as CSV. The plots and values retain the original calculations.

  • * Row-order warning: the saved comparison carries a row-order sensitivity warning.
  • † Preference change: verified reordering changed which protein model had higher alignment.
  • ! Numerical check did not pass: the comparison is displayed with its warning and should not be treated as a valid ranking.

Pending diagnostic details do not establish stability under reordering. Full diagnostic reports, protein lists, and other supporting records remain in the local research archive and are not included in this public page.
